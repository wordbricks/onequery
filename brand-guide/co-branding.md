# Co-Branding

OneQuery integrates with many tools, but the brand should remain clear: OneQuery
is the governed access layer. Partner and provider marks identify sources and
contexts; they do not become co-primary brand marks.

## Core Rule

Use partner names and logos to clarify integrations. Do not create unofficial
combined brands.

Good:

- `Connect GitHub through OneQuery`
- `Query BigQuery with cost limits`
- `Approved sources: Postgres, Linear, Sentry`

Avoid:

- `OneQuery x GitHub` as a logo lockup
- Partner logos inside the OneQuery icon tile
- Recolored partner marks unless the partner rules allow it
- Partner colors used as OneQuery page accents

## OneQuery Priority

In co-branded or integration contexts:

- OneQuery owns the page, frame, or document.
- Partner logos identify specific integrations or sources.
- The OneQuery mark should appear once in the primary brand position.
- Partner logos should be smaller, secondary, and grouped by function.

## Integration Lists

Use provider logos or names in structured lists, tables, or connector grids.

Recommended format:

| Source | Status | Policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Postgres | Connected | Read-only |
| BigQuery | Connected | Cost limit set |
| GitHub | Connected | Approved scopes |

Do not use floating logo clouds as the main proof point. OneQuery should show
controlled workflows, not just a wall of integrations.

## Partner Logo Treatment

- Preserve partner logo proportions.
- Use official partner assets when available.
- Follow partner brand rules.
- Keep enough spacing around each mark.
- Use monochrome versions only if partner rules allow them.
- Do not place partner logos on noisy or low-contrast backgrounds.

## Copy

Write integration copy in plain product language:

Good:

> Connect approved sources and run read-only queries from one governed workflow.

> Let agents query GitHub, Linear, and warehouse data through the same audit
> trail.

Avoid:

> Your favorite tools, finally united.

> The ultimate AI layer for every app you use.

## Agent And Partner Contexts

When agents use partner-connected sources, show the boundary:

- Agent requester
- OneQuery policy
- Approved source
- Query result
- Audit record

Do not show agents bypassing OneQuery to directly access partner systems unless
that is a real, intended integration behavior.

## Badges And Mentions

For docs and README badges:

- Keep badges small and functional.
- Avoid turning badge rows into the primary brand expression.
- Use badges for factual status only: license, package, CI, docs, supported
  platform.

## Do

- Use partner marks to identify data sources.
- Keep OneQuery as the governing brand.
- Follow partner logo usage rules.
- Show integration behavior through product UI and diagrams.
- Keep co-branded layouts neutral and restrained.

## Don't

- Do not create unofficial combined logos.
- Do not recolor partner logos without permission.
- Do not let partner palettes drive OneQuery pages.
- Do not imply endorsement or partnership beyond what is true.
- Do not show integrations that are not currently supported or documented.
